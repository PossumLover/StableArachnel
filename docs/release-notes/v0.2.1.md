# JamesGames v0.2.1

A new look. **Meadow** replaces the cold dark-monochrome default and the spider theme: soft, painted and floral. There's also a switch to hide adult games in the catalog. Your library, settings and Proton prefixes carry over unchanged.

## Meadow

- **New colours.** Forest sage, olive and dusty rose on warm cream, picked by hand instead of generated from one seed colour. Meadow is the default now. Existing installs switch to it once and keep their own light/dark choice.
- **Painted art**, in a light and a dusk version: rolling hills behind every page, a flowering spray and a bird around the library hero's cover, and a little round meadow for empty states.
- **New icon.** A sprout and a rose in front of the sun replaces the "J" in the side rail and is the app icon at every size, Windows `.ico` included. Small sizes get a simpler drawing so it doesn't turn to mush.
- **Nunito** is the app font.
- **Settings** is a two-column grid of cards with coloured icon badges. Appearance picks Daylight or Dusk from two painted postcards instead of a switch.
- **Game details** puts the cover, title and actions on a tinted panel with the flowering spray. Genres are coloured chips, and metadata chips no longer title-case things ("V25442602", "Source(S)").
- Stat tiles cycle sage, olive, rose and apricot instead of all being sage.
- The app says **JamesGames** everywhere now. The data folder is still `Arachnel`, so nothing moves.

## Catalog

- **Hide adult games**, on by default. It uses Steam's content descriptors: games marked *Adult Only Sexual Content* or *Frequent Nudity or Sexual Content* are hidden from the catalog and the Discover shelves. Mature games like Baldur's Gate 3, Cyberpunk 2077 and The Witcher 3 stay. The switch is in the catalog's filter sheet and applies right away.
- The first check runs in the background and takes about 10 minutes, since Steam only takes about one request a second. Until it's done a few adult games can still show up. Results are cached, so after that only new games get checked.

## For debugging

- `JamesGames --screenshot <file.png> [--page N] [--open <view>] [--delay ms]` opens a page, saves the whole window and quits.
- `scripts/dev/screenshot.sh` runs that from a sandboxed copy of your data inside headless gamescope, next to your real instance. `ARACHNEL_INSTANCE=<suffix>` gives a test copy its own single-instance name.

## Known issues

- The Russian UI shows the renamed sentences and the new adult-games switch in English until they're translated.
