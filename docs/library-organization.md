# Library organization

Search installed games by title. Use the chips below the search field to show **All games**, **Backlog**, **Playing**, **Completed**, or **Unorganized**. Choose **List** next to Favorite on a game's details page, or right-click its library card and choose a list. Assigned lists appear below the game title. Choose Unorganized to clear the assignment. Lists are saved with the library and survive updates and restarts.

Sort by Title, Recently played, or Most played. Title uses natural ordering, so Game 2 comes before Game 10. Recent and playtime sorting use Sprout's recorded sessions; unplayed games follow played games when sorting by recent activity. The chosen sort is remembered. Search and list filters are temporary and do not hide games from launch tracking or affect downloads.
